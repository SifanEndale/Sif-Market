Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I place an order?

Visit our Shop page, explore our collections, select your preferred product, fill in the required customer and order information, and submit your order request.

2. How can I ask about my order?

Contact us through email or Telegram and provide your order details so we can assist you.

3. How can I get delivery information?

Contact us to ask about delivery availability, arrangements, and any related information. Please provide your delivery address when placing your order.

4. Can I ask about product availability?

Yes! Contact us to check whether your preferred product is currently available.

5. Can I make a special request?

Of course! You can include additional comments or special requests when placing an order through our Shop page.
